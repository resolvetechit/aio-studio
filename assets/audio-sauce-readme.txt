AUDIO SAUCE - clean up voice recordings
=======================================

Run "Audio Sauce.exe". Nothing to install.

1. Drop a recording (WAV, MP3, FLAC, or an MP4 / MOV video) onto the window, or press Load.
2. Pick a starting point (there are Wedding ones) and press PLAY. The knobs change the sound while it plays.
3. Press MAKE PODCAST READY, then Export (WAV for DaVinci Resolve, or FLAC).
   Batch does many files in one go.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": the app is not code-signed yet.
Press "More info", then "Run anyway".

No sound? Press Options (top left) and choose your speakers / headphones.
